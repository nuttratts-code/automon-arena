# Shop level designs in other auto-battlers (research for set 89 · 5 Nov 2026)
From Claude's knowledge (not web-checked; patch numbers may have changed). Reference only — never copy names/art/text.

| Game | How shop level rises | Odds / gate | Gold | Interest | Streak | Reroll | Copy pool |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TFT | auto XP +2/round + buy 4 XP for 4 gold (hybrid) | odds per level, level also = board size | ~5/round + bonuses | +1 per 10, cap 5 | +1..+3 (2–5+ in a row) | 2 | shared, limited |
| Dota Underlords / Auto Chess | auto XP +1/round + buy XP (hybrid) | odds per level, level = board size | ~5/round | +1 per 10, cap 5 | small +1..+3 | 2 | shared, limited |
| Hearthstone Battlegrounds | buy tier upgrade; **cost drops 1 every turn you don't upgrade** | tier gates which units appear | 3→10 per turn, does not carry over | none | none | 1 | shared, limited |
| Super Auto Pets | **fully auto: new tier every 2 turns** | tier gates units | 10/turn fixed, no carry-over | none | none | 1 | unlimited |

Lessons for Automon Arena:
1. Games where you choose to level and gold carries over all have limited shared copies + reroll 2 + interest cap → reroll-to-Lv3-commons is blocked. We have unlimited copies + reroll 1 + uncapped interest → the set-88 finding.
2. Fully auto by turn (SAP) = simplest, fits mobile/casual; no level choice → no trap; still unlimited copies, but no carry-over gold keeps rerolling bounded.
3. Hybrid (TFT): auto pace + optional paid speed-up = a real choice without the trap of "never upgrade".
4. HS cost-drop: upgrading late is cheaper, early upgrading is a tempo gamble — removes "upgrade is never worth it".

Options for the user: A auto by round (editable table) · B hybrid auto + pay to go ahead · C keep buying, cost drops each round not upgraded. Separate lever for Lv3 commons: reroll 2 / interest cap / fewer commons at low levels.

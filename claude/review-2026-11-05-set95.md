## Findings
1. Bosses #2 and #3 are now too easy with runes (offers alone: +18 / +13 points; with the rune shop: ~89 / ~95%).
2. The rune shop is the big lever: ~35 of the ~44 runes held come from it (commons 3 gold, max 3 copies each; battle runes stack). Gold income is NOT the cause (gold per round ~27–30 vs 27 without runes).
3. Boss #1 gets HARDER (77 → 57%) when the bot buys runes early: gold goes to runes instead of units before round 5.
4. Difficulty choice barely matters (ง่าย vs ยาก k2 85 vs 90).
